# Asset credits

PosePilot uses no third-party photographs, illustrations, fonts, 3D models, textures, logos, or stock assets.

The brand mark, sculpted articulated mannequin SVGs, facial-expression illustrations, pose configurations, animation keyframes, camera guide, and written pose library are original parts of this repository. The mannequin rendering was visually informed by user-supplied pose and mannequin references. The Romantic, Editorial, Masculine full-body, expanded Feminine full-body, and Low-angle portrait collections use newly generated mannequin renders derived from those supplied references; none of the source photographs or third-party model assets are included.

Open-source runtime libraries are listed with their package metadata and licences in `package.json` and `pnpm-lock.yaml`.
